# Code of Conduct

Participants are expected to be respectful, constructive, and inclusive.
Harassment, discrimination, threats, doxxing, and deliberately disruptive
behavior are not acceptable. Maintainers may remove content or restrict
participation when necessary to keep project spaces safe.

Report conduct concerns privately to the maintainers by opening a private report
through the repository's advisory form:
https://github.com/QTSurfer/qtstreamx/security/advisories/new

Do not report conduct concerns in a public issue. Reports are handled by the
maintainers and kept confidential.
